# Triva Terms of Use

*Last updated: [date de publication]*

*This is a translation. The [French version](../conditions.md) is the authoritative one. Triva's screens are in French: the names of screens and buttons are given in English, followed by the French label in brackets the first time.*

These rules explain how to use Triva. They are short and written in plain words. By using Triva, you accept them. If you are under 15, read them with one of your parents.

## 1. What Triva is

Triva is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It searches the web, then an artificial intelligence (AI) writes an answer from the sources it found, with links to those sources.

Triva is published by **[Nom de l'éditeur]**, on a non-professional basis. Contact: **benpodro@gmail.com**.

## 2. Triva is free

Triva is **free**, with **no ads** and **no in-app purchases**. There is no account to create.

Triva has no server: the publisher does not receive your questions or your answers. Everything is explained in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## 3. Services you choose with your own key

You can connect outside services to Triva with **your own key**: a cloud AI (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Mistral…) or a search service (Brave, Tavily).

- **You** have the account with that service. You are its customer, not Triva's.
- You must **follow its terms**, including the **minimum age** it requires. For example, Tavily is only for people **18 or older**. Check the minimum age in each service's terms before creating a key.
- That service may **charge you**. Fees are between you and the service: Triva's publisher earns nothing and cannot refund them.
- Your key stays in your device's Keychain. Don't share it with anyone.
- Before the first time anything is sent to a cloud AI, Triva asks for your permission. <!-- dépend de M2 -->

## 4. Answers come from an AI

- An AI **can be wrong**: it can misread a source, mix up information or make up a detail. **Check the cited sources** before relying on an answer.
- A Triva answer **is not medical, legal or financial advice**. For those topics, ask a professional (doctor, lawyer, adviser…). In an emergency, call the emergency services (in France: 15, 17, 18 or 112).
- Answers depend on the AI engine you choose and on the pages found. The publisher does not control what they say.
- If an answer is wrong, shocking or dangerous, report it: Settings > Report a Problem (« Réglages > Signaler un problème »), or write to **benpodro@gmail.com**.

## 5. What you must not do with Triva

- Use Triva to do something illegal, to harass someone or to harm someone.
- Try to get around the protections of outside services, or use them in ways their terms don't allow.
- Present a Triva answer as a professional's advice, or as written by a human when it was generated by an AI.

## 6. Adult content filter

By default, Triva asks search engines to **filter adult content** (moderate level). You can turn this off in Settings > Advanced > Filter adult content (« Réglages > Avancé > Filtrer les contenus pour adultes »). No filter is perfect: shocking content may still get through.

## 7. Files you attach

You can attach a file to a question (PDF, text…). You are **responsible** for what you attach: only use files you have the right to use, and be careful with the personal information they contain (yours or other people's).

- The text of an attached file may be sent to the cloud AI you chose (see the privacy policy).
- **PDFs copy-protected** by their author are only read if they allow text extraction for accessibility. Otherwise, Triva doesn't read them at all, whatever the AI engine, and tells you that the file can't be read. <!-- dépend de M10 -->

## 8. Sources and links

- Triva shows excerpts, images and links from **outside websites**. That content belongs to its authors. Open the source to read the original.
- The publisher does not control those websites and is not responsible for their content.
- To read a page, Triva honestly identifies itself as "Triva" and respects websites that refuse to be read by robots (`robots.txt` file). <!-- dépend de M8 -->
- If you reuse an answer or an excerpt, respect the original author's rights (credit the source, follow its license…).
- Weather data comes from Open-Meteo, and some sources have their own license: see the [Licenses & Attributions](licenses.md) page.
